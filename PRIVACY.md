# Privacy Policy: DualSub for YouTube & Netflix

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

DualSub shows two subtitle languages at once on YouTube and Netflix. It is built to work
entirely on your device.

## What the extension does with data

- **Subtitle data.** While you watch a video on youtube.com or netflix.com, the extension reads
  that video's subtitle track list and downloads the subtitle files you select. This happens in
  your browser, using the same servers and requests the site itself uses (including YouTube's
  built-in machine translation, when you pick a language that has no native track). The text is
  held in memory only to draw the overlay and is discarded when you leave the video.
- **Settings.** Your choices (languages, sizes, colors, and so on) are saved in your browser's
  local extension storage so they persist between sessions. They are not synced to any server
  by the extension.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, browsing history, or
  viewing history.
- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics,
  tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- It does not load or run remotely hosted code.
- It does not read or modify pages other than youtube.com and netflix.com.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to save your settings locally.
- **Access to youtube.com and netflix.com** (content scripts): to read subtitle tracks and draw
  the overlay on the video player.

## Third-party services

Subtitle requests go to YouTube and Netflix, the same as when you watch normally. Their own
privacy policies apply to those requests. The extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by
YouTube, Google, or Netflix.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the extension and the
date above will change.
